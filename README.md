# Wanx Image to Video

Transform your still images into dynamic video animations with the power of AI. This tool brings your pictures to life by creating smooth transitions between two keyframes.

[中文文档](README_zh-CN.md)

## What This Tool Does

Have you ever wanted to see what happens between two moments captured in photos? This tool uses artificial intelligence to create a video that smoothly animates from one image to another. Think of it like creating a movie scene where you only provide the first and last frame, and the AI fills in all the motion in between.

## Main Features

### Key Frame to Video Generator

This is the core feature that creates animated videos from your images. Here's what it can do:

**Basic Capabilities:**
- Takes two images (a starting frame and an ending frame)
- Creates a smooth video animation that transitions between them
- Lets you describe what should happen in the video using simple text
- Produces high-quality video output in various resolutions

**What You Can Control:**

- **Starting and Ending Images**: Provide the two keyframes that define your video's beginning and end
- **Video Description**: Write a simple description of what you want to see happen (optional but recommended)
- **Quality Settings**: Choose from standard (480P), high (720P), or full HD (1080P) quality
- **Creative Control**: Tell the AI what elements to avoid in your video
- **Consistency**: Use the same settings to recreate similar results

**Smart Enhancement:**
The tool can automatically enhance your description with additional details to create more vivid and realistic animations. This helps ensure your video looks professional even if you're not sure how to describe every detail.

## How to Use It

### Basic Usage

1. **Prepare Your Images**: Have two images ready - one for the start of your video and one for the end
2. **Describe Your Vision**: Write a simple description of what should happen between these two moments
3. **Choose Your Quality**: Select the video quality that works best for your needs
4. **Generate**: Let the AI create your video

### Practical Examples

**Example 1: Character Animation**
- Start with an image of a penguin standing still
- End with an image of the same penguin in a dance pose
- Description: "A penguin performing a breakdance move"
- Result: A smooth video of the penguin transitioning into the dance

**Example 2: Scene Transformation**
- Start with a daytime landscape photo
- End with the same scene at sunset
- Description: "The sun setting over the landscape with changing colors"
- Result: A time-lapse style video showing the transition

**Example 3: Product Showcase**
- Start with a product in one position
- End with the product rotated or in a different angle
- Description: "Product rotating to show different angles"
- Result: A professional product demonstration video

### Tips for Best Results

- **Use Clear Images**: Make sure both your starting and ending images are clear and well-lit
- **Be Descriptive**: The more detail you provide in your description, the better the AI understands your vision
- **Match Your Images**: The two images should be related - showing the same subject or scene from similar perspectives works best
- **Experiment with Quality**: Start with 720P for a good balance of quality and processing time
- **Avoid Unwanted Elements**: Use the "elements to avoid" option to prevent specific things from appearing in your video

## Understanding the Results

When your video is ready, you'll receive:

- **The Video File**: Your completed animation ready to download and share
- **Original Description**: The text description you provided
- **Enhanced Description**: The detailed version the AI used (if enhancement was enabled)
- **Task Reference**: A unique identifier in case you need to reference this video later
